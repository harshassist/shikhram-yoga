Fraud Alert

Stay safe from impersonation scams.

People may pretend to be Shikhram Yoga to take money or personal details. Here is how to recognise our genuine channels.

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Shikhram Yoga will never ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, card CVV, net-banking password or remote access to your phone. Anyone who asks for these is not from us.

Our only official channels

  • Website: shikhramyoga.com — this site.
  • Email: aniket@shikhramyoga.com — messages from us come only from the @shikhramyoga.com domain.
  • WhatsApp: +91 79773 81156

Common scams to watch for

  • Fake job offers, teacher-training “placements” or franchise offers that ask for a registration or security fee.
  • Messages offering heavily discounted retreats, classes or products and asking you to pay a personal UPI ID or bank account.
  • Fake payment links, QR codes or “refund” requests that ask you to pay first or share an OTP to receive money.
  • Social media profiles or websites copying our name, logo, teachers’ photos or content.
  • Calls or messages asking you to install screen-sharing apps such as AnyDesk or TeamViewer.

How to stay safe

  • Book and pay only through this website or payment details sent from our official channels above.
  • If you are unsure, verify first by writing to aniket@shikhramyoga.com or messaging +91 79773 81156 before paying.
  • Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords with anyone.
  • Remember: to receive money, you never need to scan a QR code or enter a PIN.

If you have been targeted

  • Stop all communication and do not make further payments.
  • Call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately, or report at cybercrime.gov.in.
  • Contact your bank to block the transaction or card.
  • Let us know at aniket@shikhramyoga.com so we can warn others and report the impersonation.

Shikhram Yoga is not responsible for payments made to anyone other than our official channels, but we will help you report and act on any fraud using our name.
