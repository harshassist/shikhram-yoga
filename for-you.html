The Personal Spectrum

Designed for your body, your pace.

Every person arrives on the mat with a different body, past injuries, stress load and daily schedule. Rather than one-size-fits-all studio classes, we offer grounded, individually tailored pathways.

01 · Private & One-to-One

In-depth personal practice at your home, studio or online. Real-time adjustments, bespoke sequencing and dedicated teacher guidance.

02 · Therapeutic & Recovery

Gentle rehabilitation for back pain, postural re-alignment, joint stiffness, and stress-induced tension through mindful movement and Yin.

03 · Breathwork & Meditation

Pranayama and guided mindfulness practices to downregulate the nervous system, sharpen focus, and establish restorative daily calm.
