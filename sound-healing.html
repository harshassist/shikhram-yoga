Care before claims

A complementary practice, held responsibly.

Sound meditation is offered for relaxation and contemplative wellbeing. It does not diagnose, treat or replace professional medical care.

Please contact us before booking if you are pregnant, have sound sensitivity, epilepsy, a pacemaker or other implanted medical device, or any concern about strong sound and vibration. We can discuss comfort, positioning and whether the session is appropriate.
