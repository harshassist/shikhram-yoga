Privacy Policy

Your information, handled with care.

How Shikhram Yoga collects, uses and protects the personal information you share with us.

Last updated: 27 September 2026

1. Who we are

Shikhram Yoga (“Shikhram Yoga”, “we”, “us”) offers yoga, breathwork, meditation, sound healing, nutrition guidance, retreats, corporate wellness programmes and wellness products, online and in Pune, India. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use our website, book a session, buy from our shop or contact us.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number.
  • Booking and order details — sessions, retreats or products you choose, preferred format and schedule, and delivery address for shop orders.
  • Wellness information you choose to share — experience level, goals, injuries, health conditions, pregnancy or dietary needs, so a teacher or nutritionist can guide you safely.
  • Payment confirmation — transaction references and amounts. We do not store full card numbers, UPI PINs or bank passwords on our website.
  • Technical information — basic device, browser and usage data needed to run and secure the website.

3. How we use it

  • To confirm, schedule and deliver your sessions, retreats, programmes and orders.
  • To match you with a suitable teacher and adapt practice to your health needs.
  • To send booking confirmations, reminders and replies to your enquiries.
  • To send newsletters and offers — only if you subscribe, and you can unsubscribe anytime.
  • To prevent fraud, keep our services secure and meet legal and accounting obligations.

4. Health information

Anything you tell us about your health is used only to guide your practice or nutrition plan safely. It is shared only with the teacher or practitioner working with you and is never sold or used for advertising.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • the teacher, nutritionist or facilitator delivering your service;
  • service providers who help us operate — website hosting and database (Google Firebase), payment processing, email and messaging (including WhatsApp), and delivery partners for shop orders;
  • authorities, where the law requires it.

6. Storage and security

Your information is stored with reputable cloud providers and protected with access controls. We keep it only as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal requirements, then delete or anonymise it.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, withdraw consent, or stop marketing messages by writing to aniket@shikhramyoga.com. We respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

8. Cookies

We use only the cookies and local storage needed for the website to work, such as remembering your cart or booking progress.

9. Children

Classes for children are booked by a parent or guardian, who provides and manages the child’s information.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the date above shows the latest version. For privacy questions or complaints, contact our grievance contact at aniket@shikhramyoga.com or on WhatsApp at +91 79773 81156.
