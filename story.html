Yoga beyond the mat · Life beyond the routine

Ancient practice, thoughtfully designed for modern life.

Shikhram Yoga is a contemporary wellness brand inspired by the timeless philosophy of yoga and shaped for the realities of modern living — for individuals, families, communities and organisations.

About Shikhram Yoga

Wellness is a relationship, not a destination.

We believe wellness is not a single activity or a place you arrive at. It is an ongoing relationship between body, breath, mind and awareness.

Our approach brings together traditional yoga, meditation, breathwork, mindfulness, therapeutic movement and sound healing to create meaningful experiences for individuals and organisations — from private sessions and community programs to corporate wellness, luxury hospitality and customised retreats.

Experiences that encourage people to move consciously, breathe deeply, slow down and reconnect.

Our Vision

A world where wellness is part of everyday life.

Not something people turn to only when they are exhausted. We envision Shikhram Yoga as a trusted wellness partner, bringing authentic yogic practice into homes, workplaces, communities, hotels, resorts and retreat environments.

Our Mission

Authentic yoga, made practical and relevant.

Deliver high-quality experiences · respect the traditional roots of yoga · adapt to individual and organisational needs · encourage sustainable well-being · create meaningful experiences rather than routine classes · build a culture of awareness and conscious living.

A personal note from the founder

Yoga is more than what happens in a class.

"It is in the way we breathe when life becomes difficult. In the way we respond instead of react. In the moments when we become quiet enough to listen to ourselves."

My journey through yoga, meditation, breathwork and sound healing has shown me that every person experiences wellness differently. That understanding became the foundation of Shikhram Yoga — something that respects the depth of traditional yoga while making it relevant to today's individuals, professionals, families and organisations.

Shikhram Yoga is my effort to create spaces where people can pause, breathe, move, listen and reconnect. The goal is not to become perfect at yoga. The goal is to become more aware through yoga.
